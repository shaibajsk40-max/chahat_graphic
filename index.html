ABOUT THE ACADEMY
01

Build Skills. Create Possibilities.

Founded by Chahat Mohammadi, Creative Chahat Academy is dedicated to helping you build practical graphic design skills from basic to advanced levels.

We offer live, interactive workshops with step-by-step courses covering Canva, AI tools, and modern graphic design techniques. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to upgrade your existing skills, our structured learning approach makes it easier to learn, practice, and grow.

With 1 year of support access, you'll continue to receive guidance and assistance even after completing your course—helping you confidently apply your skills and move toward your goals.
